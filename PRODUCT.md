# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are prospective clients, collaborators, and recruiters evaluating Ahmed's capabilities and deciding whether to start a conversation about a web or mobile product.

## Product Purpose

The portfolio presents Ahmed as a full-stack web and mobile app developer, explains his capabilities and process, demonstrates selected work, and provides a reliable contact path for new opportunities.

## Positioning

The portfolio combines product-minded full-stack delivery with cross-platform mobile development, making it easy to understand both the breadth of Ahmed's implementation skills and how he works from discovery through launch and support.

## Operating Context

Visitors may arrive from search, a social profile, or a direct referral and will evaluate the site on phones and desktop screens. The primary workflow is scanning the work and services, checking credibility, and sending a project inquiry.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The site is a single-page portfolio with supporting metadata and error/loading routes.
- Content is maintained in typed data files under `src/data/`.
- The stack is Next.js App Router, TypeScript strict mode, Tailwind CSS v4, and server components by default.
- The contact form must validate on the client and server, send email through Gmail SMTP, and include anti-spam protections.
- The site must support polished dark and light themes, responsive layouts, keyboard accessibility, reduced motion, and strong performance.
- Placeholder content is allowed where real portfolio materials are unavailable and must be marked with `TODO(ahmed)`.

## Brand Commitments

- The name used in the interface is Ahmed.
- The requested visual direction is a premium dark twilight aesthetic with deep navy-black surfaces, teal, coral, and violet accents, glass-like depth, crisp typography, and generous whitespace.
- The default theme is dark while respecting the visitor's system preference on first visit.

## Evidence on Hand

No existing portfolio content, images, resume, testimonials, project case studies, or brand assets were present in the workspace at initialization. Future placeholder claims must be clearly marked for replacement.

## Product Principles

- Make the work and the next action obvious within the first viewport.
- Show credible delivery range without overwhelming the visitor.
- Keep content editable without requiring component changes.
- Preserve access, performance, and graceful degradation as first-class product requirements.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The site must be keyboard accessible, maintain WCAG AA contrast for body content in both themes, provide visible focus states, use semantic landmarks and labels, support reduced motion, and remain readable and usable from 320px-wide screens upward.
